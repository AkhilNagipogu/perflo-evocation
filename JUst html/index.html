FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

What is a subdomain?

A custom hostname attached to a parent domain, such as project.evocation.in.

Do I get website hosting?

No. This demo is focused on subdomain rental and DNS management. Users connect their subdomains to external services.

Can I manage DNS records?

Yes. The dashboard includes a simulated DNS editor for A, AAAA, CNAME, and TXT records.

How do wallet credits work?

Users add prepaid credits to a wallet, and rental charges are deducted from that wallet in the demo.

What happens when my rental expires?

The rental enters an expired state, and the name may be changed or reactivated based on the available demo rules.

Can I change my subdomain?

Yes, the demo supports changing a name after releasing the prior assignment and checking mock availability.

What happens when my wallet runs out?

Add credits before activating another rental.
