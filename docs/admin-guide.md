# Administrator Operations Manual

## 1. Accessing the Admin Console

The administrative dashboard is located at `https://admin.example.com` (or `/admin` on local setups).
Access requires an account in `admin_users` with role `super_admin`, `admin`, or `support`.

Default Initial Credentials (generated by `scripts/seed_data.py`):
- **Email**: `admin@example.com`
- **Password**: Configured in `.env` (`ADMIN_INITIAL_PASSWORD`) or default `AdminSecure123!`
> **Important**: Change your administrative password immediately upon initial deployment.

---

## 2. Managing Subdomain Rentals

In the **Subdomain Rentals** tab:
- **Search**: Locate rentals by FQDN (e.g. `test.example.com`) or customer UUID.
- **Extend**: Add extra days (e.g. 30 days) to a rental's `expires_at` without charging the customer's wallet.
- **Suspend**: Immediately deactivates Cloudflare DNS records and sets status to `SUSPENDED` (used during abuse investigations).
- **Restore**: Reactivates suspended DNS records in Cloudflare and marks rental `ACTIVE`.
- **Force Release**: Lapses a domain immediately, deleting all DNS records and opening the name for re-registration.

---

## 3. Financial Controls & Adjustments

In the **Wallet Adjustments** tab:
1. Provide the target customer's UUID.
2. Specify the adjustment amount in INR (positive number to credit, negative number to debit).
3. **Provide an explicit audit reason** (mandatory).
4. Committing the adjustment executes an atomic transaction in `wallet_transactions` and writes an immutable record to `audit_logs`.

---

## 4. Abuse Report Triage

In the **Abuse Queue** tab:
1. Review incoming incident reports submitted by the public.
2. View the reported domain, category (phishing, malware, etc.), description, and reporter contact.
3. Click **Suspend Domain** to immediately suspend the rental and deactivate its Cloudflare DNS records.
4. Click **Dismiss** if the report is invalid or malicious.

---

## 5. Audit Trail Verification

The **Audit Trail** tab displays an immutable sequence of all administrative interventions, system events, and policy adjustments:
- Timestamp (UTC)
- Actor email and type (`admin`, `system`)
- Action and affected resource
- Detailed before/after JSON states
